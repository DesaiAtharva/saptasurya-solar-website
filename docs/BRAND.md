# Saptasurya Solar Energy — Brand Guide

## The mark: "Sapta Rashmi" (seven rays)

**Saptasurya** = *Sapta* (seven) + *Surya* (sun). The mark is a sun rising over the
horizon with **exactly seven rays**. The face of the sun is divided like a **solar
panel**, and the two lines below are the horizon / rooftop.

Meaning: *a new dawn of free, clean energy for every home.*

Always keep **seven** rays — that is the meaning of the name.

## Wordmark

"**Saptasurya**" in Plus Jakarta Sans ExtraBold (800), tight letter-spacing (−2%), brand navy.
"**SOLAR ENERGY**" below in Plus Jakarta Sans Bold (700), uppercase, letter-spacing +18–22%, sun-orange.
Place the wordmark to the right of the mark, vertically centred.

## Rules

- Minimum size: 16 px (screen) / 8 mm (print). Below 24 px drop the wordmark.
- Clear space around the mark: at least the height of one ray on every side.
- Don't stretch, rotate, recolour the rays, add shadows/gradients, or change the number of rays.

## Before trademark registration

The mark was created as an original design. Before filing:
1. Search **IP India Public Search** (https://tmrsearch.ipindia.gov.in/eregister/) for the word
   "SAPTASURYA" (and "SAPTA SURYA") in Classes **9, 11, 37, 42**.
2. Do a **device (logo) search** under Vienna category **01.03 (Sun)** — especially
   **01.03.01 (sun rising or setting)** — in the same classes.
3. Have a trademark attorney / designer finalise vector outlines (convert the wordmark to paths).
